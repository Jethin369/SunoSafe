03 / How it works

Transparent by design
01 · Transcribe

Gnani Prisma converts supported audio into text. Transcription errors can affect subsequent analysis.

02 · Examine

Transparent phrase-pattern checks inspect the transcript. Evidence cards show phrases that match configured checks.

03 · Respond

Practical safety guidance and optional Gnani Timbre speech output help users understand possible next steps.

SunoSafe is a prototype for scam-awareness education.
It does not authenticate callers, guarantee detection, or replace professional advice.
